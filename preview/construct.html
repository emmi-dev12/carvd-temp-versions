construction

the ride is in the spec.

a custom board is not a graphic on a blank. construction is the product. sintered base, engineered core, ceramic edges, no-voc epoxy, locked in a full cure and a cold press.

corepaulownia, bamboo, poplar, cork, carbon, honeycomb, titanium. flex from laminate placement, not only thickness.
fibershigh-stitch triax glass. pre-cured carbon where the ride needs it.
dampingthree layers of rubber / vds.
sidewallspu, sintered p-tex, or bamboo. not commodity abs.
edges48 rockwell (40 for rails). ceramic disk, a mirror finish.
basessintered p-tex 4000 and electra. a hard extruded base only if asked.
epoxyelastomer, zero voc.
pressfull cure, then cold press so the shape holds.
finishmany light grinds. die-cuts you cannot feel.
graphicssublimation, screen, die-cut, printed veneer, water transfer, digital. gloss or matte.
